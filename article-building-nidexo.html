From Real-World Experience to Nidexo: Building the AI of Tomorrow

Marouen Ben Sghaier • Founder of Nidexo • August 2026 • 5 min read

Every ambitious project takes time to take shape. Nidexo was not born from a passing idea or a simple trend. It is the result of more than ten years of experience in digital communication and digital transformation. Seeing Nidexo come to life today feels like welcoming something new into the world. It is a project driven by passion, shaped by patience, and designed from day one with an international ambition.

My experience working with public institutions and businesses exposed me to a recurring challenge: how to provide responsive and personalized customer service without exhausting valuable resources. But the real spark came from a daily conversation at home. By sharing experiences with my wife, who works directly in SME financing and credit, we began connecting our perspectives. We identified not only a communication challenge, but also its direct financial impact on business growth.

Nidexo was born at the intersection of communication strategy and real-world business challenges. We are building an artificial intelligence platform that goes beyond automating responses, helping businesses build stronger customer relationships and support their growth. Designed with ambitious international standards in mind, Nidexo is built to cross borders and serve businesses wherever they operate.
